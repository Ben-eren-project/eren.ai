Questions

The honest answers.

Is the first conversation really free?

Yes — a 15-minute call to see whether there's a question worth digging into, no charge and no obligation. It sits outside the packages. The paid work starts at Diagnose, and only if it's a fit.

What do I get with Diagnose?

One problem or customer journey examined against your real evidence: a map of how it works today, up to three ranked fixes each with a way to measure it, an action plan, a scope and price where feasible for anything we could build, and a 45-minute walkthrough. It's a decision pack you own — useful whether or not you hire us to build.

Is the Diagnose fee taken off the Build price?

No — they're separate. Diagnose is diagnosis and planning; Build is implementation. A $750 diagnosis followed by a $2,000 build is $2,750 total. Keeping them separate means the diagnosis stands on its own and never becomes unpaid sales work.

Do I have to commit to anything ongoing?

No. Diagnose and Build are both one-time — no subscriptions or retainers, and nothing ongoing to commit to.

Who's actually doing the work?

Eren (built on Claude) runs the analysis, a second model independently checks it for unsupported claims, and a person reviews and approves everything before it reaches you. Nothing is sent automatically.

What happens to my information?

It's used only to analyze your business. To do that, it's processed by the AI models Eren runs on — it is never sold or shared for marketing.

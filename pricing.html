Frequently
asked questions

The honest answers. Still unsure? Ask on the free First Look.

What can a Build include?

Anything from one focused fix to a new website with a proper backend, your tools connected, custom software, or a supervised AI workflow. The diagnosis and your goals decide which. AI goes in only where it fits, and a person approves what it drafts before anything goes out.

How is a Build priced?

From $1,500, with one fixed price agreed before work starts. Half to start, half when you accept it. Software, hosting, ads and tax are always shown separately before you agree.

Do we have to replace the software we have?

No. We start with what you already use. Often it just needs setting up properly or connecting. We suggest replacing something only when that clearly beats fixing it, and you decide.

What if something breaks after handover?

For 30 days after you accept a Build, anything that stops meeting a check we agreed is fixed at no charge. If one of your other tools changes later, adapting to it is new work, quoted separately, unless our work caused the problem within those 30 days. We point out which tools that applies to before you agree the quote.

What if I cancel partway through a Build?

You pay for the work done so far, never more than the 50% deposit, and we refund the rest of the deposit.

Is the First Look really free?

Yes. It’s a 15-minute call, with no charge and no obligation. Paid work starts at Diagnose, and only if it’s a fit.

What does Diagnose cost, and what do I get?

$750, or $495 for the first five paying clients. You get one problem checked against your real evidence: how it works today, up to three ranked fixes with a way to measure each, an action plan, a scope and price where feasible for anything we could build, and a 45-minute walkthrough. It’s yours to keep, whether or not you hire us to build.

Is the Diagnose fee taken off the Build price?

No, they’re separate. A $750 Diagnose followed by a $2,000 Build is $2,750 in total. Keeping them separate means the diagnosis stands on its own.

How is this different from ChatGPT, and what happens to my information?

ChatGPT answers the question you ask, and for some jobs that’s enough. We’ll tell you when. Eren, built on Claude, starts from the evidence you share and works out which question matters. A second model checks the findings for unsupported claims, independently when one is available, and Ben reviews everything before it reaches you. Nothing is sent automatically. Your information is used only to analyze your business. It’s processed by the AI models Eren runs on and never sold or shared for marketing.

How we bake

Fifteen hours from flour to shelf.

That’s why there’s one bake a day, and why we ask for orders by 8 PM: they’re counted out at 3 AM, before anything goes on the shelf.

1

The day before, 4 PM

Mix

Flour, water, salt and starter. The dough rests, then gets four folds over the evening.

2

Overnight, about 16 hours

Cold rise

Into the cooler. Slow and cold is where the flavour and the open crumb come from.

3

3 AM

Shape

Divided, shaped by hand and scored. Orders are counted out first, then the shelf.

4

4 AM

Bake

Two decks, steam for the first fifteen minutes, then dark.

5

7 AM

On the shelf

Ordered loaves go in named bags behind the counter. The rest go on the shelf until they’re gone.

Questions

Before you come in.

Do you sell out?

Most days, yes. Bread is baked once, overnight, so when the shelf is empty we’re done for the day. Ordering ahead is the only way to be sure.

How long do you hold an order?

Until 1 PM on your pickup day. After that it goes on the shelf, so let us know if you’re running late.

Can I order for an event?

Yes, with three days’ notice. Use the order form and put the details in the note.

Do you make gluten-free bread?

No. Everything here uses wheat or rye flour and is made on the same bench, so we can’t offer anything gluten-free.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, until 8 PM the night before. After that the dough is already mixed.

The decisions behind this page

For the walkthrough. A client site wouldn’t carry this panel.

  1. The home page answers three questions: what the shop makes, who it’s for, and how to start. The detail sits one click away on its own page.
  2. Three customers, three paths. The invented “before” had one services list mixing laser cutting, railings and repairs. Here each kind of visitor picks their own job and lands on what to send.
  3. The new tube laser is on the assemblies tag, not in a news post. It shows again in the limits table.
  4. “How a job runs” is summarised here, because change handling is what customers worry about. The full version, and where the Change Desk workflow demo connects, is on How we work.
  5. “Start a quote” is in the header on every page, so nobody has to hunt for it.
  6. No stock praise, testimonials or invented numbers. In a fictional demo, anything that looks like proof would be made up.

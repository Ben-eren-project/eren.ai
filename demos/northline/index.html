Which kind of job is yours?

Each one has its own way in, and says what to send.

  • For engineers and buyers

    Production parts to print

    Repeat orders of cut, formed and welded parts, with the drawing revision locked on every order.

    Send usA drawing, the material and a quantity

  • For maintenance teams

    Repair and replacement parts

    Guards, brackets, chutes and frames when a machine is down and the original supplier is gone.

    Send usA photo, rough sizes and where it mounts

  • For builders and installers

    Fabricated assemblies

    Railings, frames, enclosures and tube structures, with shop drawings to approve before anything is cut.

    Send usDrawings or a site sketch, and the finish

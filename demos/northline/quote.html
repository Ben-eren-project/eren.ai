Send what you have. We’ll ask for the rest.

A drawing and quantity are enough to start. With only a photo and rough sizes, choose “Repair or replacement part” and we’ll sketch it for you to confirm.

What helps us quote

  • Material, and thickness or gauge if you know it
  • Quantity, now and per year if it repeats
  • When you need it
  • Finish: bare, painted or powder coated

What happens after you send it

  1. Your coordinator reads it and replies by email, with questions or a written quote.
  2. If it’s outside the shop’s limits, you hear that plainly instead of silence.
  3. Nothing is cut until you approve the quote, and the drawings for assemblies.

You’ll know who has your job. And what happens if it changes.

Every job has one coordinator from quote to delivery. Below is how a job moves through the shop, how changes are handled, and what the machines can and can’t do.

How a job runs.

Four steps, with the same coordinator in each one.

  1. 1

    Quote

    We confirm material, quantity and finish, and ask for anything missing before we price it. You get a written quote with the date it’s based on.

    You send: The request

  2. 2

    Approve, confirmed with you in writing

    For assemblies you approve shop drawings. For parts, the drawing revision is locked on the order.

    You send: Your approval

  3. 3

    Make

    Your coordinator sends first-article photos on request and tells you straight away if anything affects price or date.

    You send: Nothing, unless asked

  4. 4

    Change or deliver, confirmed with you in writing

    Changes come in writing. Cost and date are confirmed before the floor acts on them.

    You send: The change, in writing

Confirmed with you in writing

Changes are written down before they reach the floor.

A new quantity or drawing revision needs a clear handoff. Nothing changes on the floor until three things are settled.

  • The new quantity, drawing or finish, in writing
  • The cost: unchanged, or a new price you’ve accepted
  • The date: unchanged, or a new date you’ve agreed

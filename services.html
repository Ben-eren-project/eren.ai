Three ways to improve how the work gets done.

The diagnosis and your priorities set the scope. Each Build has a fixed price, agreed checks and a clear handover.

Website restructuring and redesign

If this sounds familiar: “Our site doesn’t say what we actually do any more.” “People can’t find the service they came for.”

You get
The agreed pages rebuilt around the customers you serve, written from verified facts, working on a phone, with one clear next step and an inquiry route that reaches the right person. Plus an editing guide and a handover sheet.
Typical checks
Your services are described accurately, a test reader finds the right offer, the inquiry route works end to end, and you can make routine edits yourself.
Not included
Guaranteed leads or rankings, invented reviews or badges, or taking over disputed domains and accounts.

See a fictional demo: Northline Metal WorksA four-page site for a fabrication shop, with a quote form built around what an estimator needs.

Workflow and connected tools

If this sounds familiar: “Changes get lost between the office and the team.” “We enter the same thing three times.”

You get
One process mapped from a real example, then the simplest reliable solution that fits the job and its ongoing costs: your current software set up properly, two tools connected where their plans allow, or a small tool when nothing you have will do. Your data stays yours and exportable.
Typical checks
The normal case, the missing-information case and the exception each behave as agreed, nothing happens without the approval you set, and your staff can run it without us.
Not included
Connections we haven’t confirmed your plan allows, replacing software that works, or anything that sends, pays or decides on its own.

See a fictional demo: Change DeskA change-request tool for the same shop. Nothing reaches the floor until cost, date and confirmation are settled.

Practical AI inside one defined job

If this sounds familiar: “Staff keep asking the same questions.” “We want a first draft from our own records.”

You get
AI with one job, one approved set of sources and a named person who checks it. For example, an assistant that answers only from your procedures and shows where each answer came from.
Typical checks
A fixed set of test questions, including missing and trick ones, has been run and the results written down, and your reviewer signs off.
Not included
Anything that acts on its own, or promises about accuracy before the test set exists.

Every Build: one fixed price before work starts, half to start and half when you accept it, and a 30-day fix period after that. Everything built is yours, with no subscription to us. Software, hosting, ads and tax are always shown separately before you agree.

How every finding is checked

Every claim has to show how we know it.

Findings are labeled by where the evidence came from, checked independently, and reviewed by a person. Anything that can't be backed up doesn't make it into your pack.

Observed

Something we saw directly on your site, listings, or tools.

"No hours on the business profile."

Tested

Something we tried with your permission, like a test call or form.

"Test inquiry never reached a person."

Asked

Something you told us, recorded as yours, not presented as proven.

"Owner: nobody checks that inbox."

Unknown

Something we couldn't confirm. Written down, with how to find out.

"Weekly inquiry count: not yet known."
Thrown out
"You're losing 40% of your leads."
A confident number with nothing behind it. It doesn't reach you.
Kept
"A test call at 9:47 PM got no callback."
Specific, checked, and something you can act on.

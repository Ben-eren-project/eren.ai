Diagnosis · example · illustrative, not a real client

The job changed.
The team needs the same version.

  1. Prioritized for your situation. The recommendation weighs likely impact, evidence, effort and dependencies.
  2. Evidence on every finding. Observed, tested, or something you told us.
  3. Unknowns written down. Labeled, with a next step to check them.
  4. A check for each change. An acceptance check, and an outcome measure where records are available.
01

Two versions of the job are in use.

Evidence
ObservedThe office record shows the revised scope; the team’s handover still shows the earlier version.
Fix
Keep the approved scope in one shared job record and link the handover to it.
Measure
A test revision appears in the record the team actually uses.
02

The approval is separate from the work record.

Evidence
ObservedThe example approval is in an email, with no reference attached to the job.
Fix
Record who approved the change and link the confirmation before releasing it.
Measure
A change without the required approval stays on hold.
03

The same update is entered twice.

Evidence
AskedThe fictional owner describes copying each change into a second tool. The available integration has not been checked.
Fix
Confirm what the existing plans support before scoping a connection or custom tool.
Measure
The agreed normal case and exception case pass an end-to-end test.
